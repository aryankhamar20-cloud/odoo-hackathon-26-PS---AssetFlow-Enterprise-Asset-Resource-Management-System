Audit Cycles

REF — not yet in live app

Also mined from Dhruv's stray push — AppContext.jsx has a complete working implementation: createAuditCycle (scope by department or location, seeds an "Unchecked" checklist), checkAuditAsset (mark each asset Verified / Missing / Damaged, auto-builds a discrepancy report), and closeAuditCycle (any asset still marked Missing gets auto-flipped to Lost status — reuses the same terminal-status mechanism as our live Asset Status Control). This is the #1 item on our cut-scope list and it's basically already designed — just needs porting from React/localStorage into a Next.js server action + Supabase table.
